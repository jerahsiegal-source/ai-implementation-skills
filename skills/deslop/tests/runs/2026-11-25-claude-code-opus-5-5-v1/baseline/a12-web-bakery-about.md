# Hearth & Crumb

## A family bakery since 2012

We started in 2012 with one oven, a mixer we borrowed, and a lot of early mornings. Portland has kept us going ever since.

## What we bake

Mostly bread. We mix each loaf's dough the day before and let it rise slowly overnight, which gives it more flavor. We also make croissants, scones, seasonal pies, and cookies, which are usually gone by noon.

We bake with real butter, local flour when we can get it, and whatever fruit is in season. We don't cut corners. If we wouldn't serve it at our own table, we won't sell it to you.

## Who we are

We're a family business. The people who mix the dough in the morning also unlock the front door and ask how your week's going. Some regulars have been coming in since our first year, and now their kids are the ones at the pastry case pointing at what they want.

## Stop by

Get a coffee and something fresh out of the oven, and stay for a while. Or take a loaf home for dinner.

We're glad you found us.
