Just wrapped my first year as an engineering manager. A few things I learned, in case they help someone else making the same jump:

1. Talk to your team a lot, and listen more than you talk. Regular, honest conversations built trust faster than anything else I tried, and the results followed.

2. Delegating isn't just getting work off your plate. Done right, it's how your engineers grow. Seeing people on my team take real ownership has been one of the best parts of the job.

3. Stay close to the code, but don't hide in it. Most of my impact came from unblocking people and setting up conditions where good work could happen.

4. Give feedback early and make it specific and useful. Ask for it too. Asking for feedback myself has done more for my growth than almost anything else.

5. You never stop learning. Something new comes up every day, and each one is a chance to get a bit better at this.

Grateful for the team I get to work with.
