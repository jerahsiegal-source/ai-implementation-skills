**Orbis Freight Opens Rotterdam Hub**

Orbis Freight opened its Rotterdam hub on 14 October 2025. The 38,000-square-metre facility has 220 staff. We are targeting 1.2 million parcels per week by Q2 2026.

The hub cost €46.5 million. Because of its design, CO2 emissions per parcel are 18% lower than at our Antwerp site.
