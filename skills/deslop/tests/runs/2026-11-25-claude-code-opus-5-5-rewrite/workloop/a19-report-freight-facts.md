**Orbis Freight Opens Rotterdam Hub**

On 14 October 2025 we opened our Rotterdam hub, a new site in our European network. It covers 38,000 square metres and has 220 staff.

We put €46.5 million into it. Because of the way the hub is designed, each parcel that goes through it produces 18% less CO2 than at our Antwerp site.
