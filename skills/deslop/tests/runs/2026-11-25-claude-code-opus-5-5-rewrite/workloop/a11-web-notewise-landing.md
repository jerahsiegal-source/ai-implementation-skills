# Notewise

Notewise takes the notes in your meetings, so you can talk instead of scribble. It pulls out the decisions and action items, plus any open questions.

[Start free, no card needed]

---

**Notes**
Notewise joins the call and transcribes every word. Within seconds, it turns the conversation into clean, structured notes.

**Action items**
Each commitment gets an owner and a due date. Notewise then sends it to Slack, Asana, Jira or Google Calendar.

**Search**
Ask "What did we decide about pricing in March?" and you get the answer, with a link to the exact moment someone said it.

**Where it works**
Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and meetings in the same room.

**Privacy**
Recordings are encrypted, and we never use them to train models. You choose who sees each note. Delete anything, anytime.
