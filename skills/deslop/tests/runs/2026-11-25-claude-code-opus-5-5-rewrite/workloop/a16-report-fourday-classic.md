We ran a four-day work week for 12 weeks with 46 employees from our Denver office.

Teams got as much done on a four-day schedule: output per person stayed flat throughout the pilot. Sick days fell from 61 to 38. Of the 46 people, 41 (roughly 89%) asked to keep the four-day schedule.
