# Billing Migration Status

**Started:** March 3, 2025
**Launch target:** November 15, 2025
**Status:** In progress, one open risk

We're moving billing from Stripe Billing v1 to Chargebee. Phases 1–3 of 5 are done (60%), and we've spent $71,500 of the $120,000 budget (59.6%), so spending is keeping pace with the work. That leaves phases 4 and 5 and $48,500. The biggest threat to the launch date is rebuilding the invoice templates.

## Invoice templates

The Stripe Billing v1 invoices have to be rebuilt as Chargebee templates. If that work runs late, it could hold up the last two phases and the November 15 launch.

Our plan is to put it first in the next phase. We'll go over the template requirements early and get sign-off from the people who depend on the invoices.

## Next steps

1. Rebuild the invoice templates.
2. Finish phases 4 and 5.
3. Keep spending within the remaining $48,500.
4. Get ready for the November 15, 2025 launch.

## Decisions needed

None right now. We'll raise one if the invoice work starts to threaten the launch date or the budget.
