# Notewise

Notewise takes notes in your meetings, so you can talk instead of scribbling. It writes down the decisions and action items, and lists the questions nobody answered.

[Start free, no card needed]

---

**Automatic notes**
Notewise joins your call and transcribes every word. Within seconds you have clean, structured notes.

**Action items that don't get lost**
Every commitment gets an owner and a due date. Notewise then sends it to Slack, Asana, Jira or Google Calendar.

**Search every meeting you've ever had**
Ask "What did we decide about pricing in March?" and get the answer, with a link to the exact moment someone said it.

**Works with your calls**
Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus in-person conversations.

**Private by default**
Recordings are encrypted and never used to train models. You choose who sees each note, and you can delete anything at any time.
