**Orbis Freight Opens Rotterdam Hub**

Orbis Freight opened its Rotterdam hub on 14 October 2025. It is the newest site in our European network. The building covers 38,000 square metres and has 220 staff.

The hub cost €46.5 million. Its design cuts CO2 emissions per parcel by 18% compared with our Antwerp site.
