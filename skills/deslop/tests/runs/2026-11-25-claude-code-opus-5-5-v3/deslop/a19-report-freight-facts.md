**Orbis Freight Opens Rotterdam Hub**

Orbis Freight opened its Rotterdam hub on 14 October 2025, adding a new site to our European network. The facility covers 38,000 square metres and employs 220 staff.

The hub cost €46.5 million. Its design cuts CO2 emissions per parcel by 18% compared with our Antwerp site.
