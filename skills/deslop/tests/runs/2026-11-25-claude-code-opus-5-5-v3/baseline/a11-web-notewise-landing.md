# Notewise

**Meeting notes, decisions and to-dos, written for you.**

Notewise joins your meeting and takes the notes. It lists the decisions, the action items and the questions nobody answered, so you can pay attention to the conversation.

**Notes in seconds**
Notewise joins the call, transcribes all of it, and turns it into organized notes a few seconds after you hang up.

**Every action item has an owner**
When someone agrees to do something, Notewise records who it is and when it's due. It then sends the item to Slack, Asana, Jira or Google Calendar.

**Search all your past meetings**
Type a question like "What did we decide about pricing in March?" You'll get the answer and a link to the point in the recording where someone said it.

**Zoom, Meet, Teams or in person**
Notewise works with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. It also records meetings held in person.

**Your recordings stay private**
Recordings are encrypted and are never used to train models. You decide who can see each note, and you can delete anything at any time.
