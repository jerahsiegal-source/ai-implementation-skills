May 21 is Kids to Parks Day, which gets kids out into nature to explore, learn, and play.

What's a tech team doing talking about the outdoors? Last year we [designed and built](https://18f.gsa.gov/2015/09/03/every-kid-in-a-park/) the Every Kid in a Park website, a project that put modern tech to work for ancient forests and historic sites. So we're glad to have a reason to plug it again.

Every Kid in a Park and Kids to Parks Day want the same thing: families outside, seeing natural wonders and historic sites in person.

Maybe you've been to dozens of parks, or maybe you've never been to one. Either way, May is a good month to plan a trip. If there's a fourth grader in your family, the whole thing is free. Fourth graders can sign up now at [everykidinapark.gov](https://www.everykidinapark.gov).
