Here are the results of our four-day work week pilot. It ran for 12 weeks with 46 employees from the Denver office.

- **Productivity:** Output per person stayed flat across the pilot, so teams kept up their performance on the shorter schedule.
- **Sick days:** These fell from 61 to 38, which suggests people were healthier and better rested.
- **Support for keeping it:** 41 of the 46 participants (about 89%) asked to stay on the four-day schedule.

Productivity held up, people took fewer sick days and most participants want to keep the schedule. Next, we'll look at whether the model can keep working for the business and for our staff.
