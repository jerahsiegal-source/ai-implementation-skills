Yesterday the Government Accountability Office (GAO) published [its review](http://gao.gov/products/GAO-16-602) of 18F and the U.S. Digital Service, and of our work to change how federal digital services are built. We're glad GAO took a close look at 18F's work and mission. The part we liked most was a customer satisfaction survey GAO ran as part of the review. GAO surveyed agencies 18F has worked with. Of the 26 that responded, 23 said they were happy with our work.

Our partners' opinions matter a lot to us. We read these results as good evidence that we're on track to have the effect on federal technology that we want.

The report also named places where 18F can do better. GAO said we should set goals based on outcomes and create performance measures tied to reaching full cost recovery. We agree with these recommendations, and we'll put policies and procedures in place to act on them.

Government works well only with careful, thorough oversight. Thanks to GAO for working with 18F over the past year on this review. Their work helps us stay transparent and accountable, which we need in order to serve the federal government and the public well.
