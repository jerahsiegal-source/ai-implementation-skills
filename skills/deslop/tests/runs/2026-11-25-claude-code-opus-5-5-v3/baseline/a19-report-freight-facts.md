**Orbis Freight Opens Rotterdam Hub**

Orbis Freight opened its Rotterdam hub on 14 October 2025, adding a new site to our European network. The facility covers 38,000 square metres and employs 220 staff.

We invested €46.5 million in the hub. Because of its design, CO2 emissions per parcel are 18% lower than at our Antwerp site.

We work with Maersk and DB Schenker across the supply chain, which lets us offer customers faster and more reliable deliveries. Our target is 1.2 million parcels per week by Q2 2026.

CEO Marta Lindqvist leads the business, and the Rotterdam team is driving much of our current growth. Thank you to our customers, partners and colleagues who helped with the launch.
