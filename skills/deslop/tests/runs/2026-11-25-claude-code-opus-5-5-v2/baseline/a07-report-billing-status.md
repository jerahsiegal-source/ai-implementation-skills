# Stripe Billing v1 → Chargebee migration: status

Started March 3, 2025. Target launch is November 15, 2025. The project is in progress, and there is one risk to flag.

## Where we are

Phases 1–3 are done, so we're 3 of 5 phases (60%) through. We've spent $71,500 of the $120,000 budget (59.6%), which tracks with the work completed. That leaves $48,500 (40.4%) for phases 4 and 5.

## Risk: invoice templates

Chargebee's invoice templates have to be rebuilt to replace the Stripe Billing v1 invoices. If this slips, phases 4 and 5 slip with it, and so could the November 15 launch.

Our plan is to make this the first priority in the next phase. We'll go through the template requirements early and get sign-off from the stakeholders who depend on invoice output.

## Next

1. Finish the invoice template rework.
2. Complete phases 4 and 5.
3. Stay within the remaining $48,500.
4. Get ready for the November 15, 2025 launch.

No decisions are needed from anyone right now. We'll come back with a decision request if the template work starts to threaten the launch date or the budget.
