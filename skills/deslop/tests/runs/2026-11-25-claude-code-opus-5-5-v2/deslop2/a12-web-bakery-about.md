# Hearth & Crumb

## Baked by family, since 2012

We opened Hearth & Crumb in 2012 with one oven, a borrowed mixer, and a lot of early mornings. Portland has been feeding us ever since.

## What we make

Bread comes first. Every loaf starts the day before, with a slow-rising dough that gets time to develop its flavor. Alongside it you'll find croissants, scones, seasonal pies, and cookies that sell out before noon.

We use real butter, local flour when we can get it, and fruit that's in season. We don't use shortcuts, and we don't make anything we wouldn't serve at our own table.

## Who we are

Hearth & Crumb is a family business, and we mean that literally. The people who mix the dough are the people who open the doors, and they're the people who will ask how your week is going. Some of our regulars have been coming in since the first year. Their kids now point at the pastry case and tell us what they want.

## Come say hello

Stop in for a coffee and something warm from the oven. Pull up a chair, or take a loaf home for dinner.

We're glad you found us.
